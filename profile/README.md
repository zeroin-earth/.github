# AI Use at Little Thicket Studios (ZeroIn)

AI is a tool. It is accessibility.

It is the elevator for the stairs. It is the car for the horse.

Little Thicket Studios will always rely on humans for their creativity, ideas, and hard work. All decisions, all final code reviews, all future plans, and all verified research. Every brush stroke, pen scribble, composed note, whiteboard mark, every word of story and dialogue, and every bug must be made by humans.

We currently have safeguards in place that instruct AI never to contribute any authored creative or written work to the codebase or marketing materials. All areas needing a human touch are marked and reviewed with every build. All visual and audio assets must be attested by our partner artists as original works.

But we fully acknowledge that sometimes you can only use the tool.

Times like prototyping, for fear of taking too long and not working.

Memory support for keeping track of long-running tasks that simply do not fit inside a human brain.

Writing tooling to help do our jobs.

Building automations for mundane, daily tasks.

Cognitive offloading.

These are all examples of using a tool as a tool. Cutting wood with a table saw instead of a hand saw. Tightening a bolt with a wrench instead of by hand. And, like most tools, it is very difficult to go back to life before you had one, but at Little Thicket Studios, we will always strive never to lose sight of where the amazing parts happen. Where we came from.

And we will never automate the amazing parts away.
